### **Week 1: Number Theory & Modular Arithmetic**  
- **Topics**:  
  - Prime factorization (Sieve of Eratosthenes, Pollard’s Rho).  
  - Modular inverses, Fermat’s Little Theorem, Euler’s Totient Theorem.  
  - Chinese Remainder Theorem (CRT), Lucas Theorem for binomial coefficients.  
  - Diophantine equations (Extended Euclidean Algorithm).  
- **Practice**:  
  - [CSES Problems](https://cses.fi/problemset/task/1713) (Prime Divisors, Exponentiation II).  
  - [Project Euler](https://projecteuler.net/) (Problems 5, 48, 108).  

---

### **Week 2: Combinatorics**  
- **Topics**:  
  - Permutations, combinations, inclusion-exclusion principle.  
  - Derangements, Catalan numbers, Stirling numbers.  
  - Stars and Bars, Burnside’s Lemma (group theory in combinatorics).  
- **Practice**:  
  - [LeetCode Combinatorics](https://leetcode.com/tag/combinatorics/) (e.g., 62, 96).  
  - [Codeforces Problems](https://codeforces.com/problemset?tags=combinatorics) (e.g., 1312D, 1513E).  

---

### **Week 3: Algebra & Polynomials**  
- **Topics**:  
  - Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) for polynomial multiplication.  
  - Matrix exponentiation (solving linear recurrences like Fibonacci).  
  - Gaussian Elimination, determinant calculations.  
  - Generating functions (for counting problems).  
- **Practice**:  
  - [SPOJ POLYMUL](https://www.spoj.com/problems/POLYMUL/) (FFT).  
  - [CSES Dice Combinations](https://cses.fi/problemset/task/1633/) (Matrix exponentiation).  

---

### **Week 4: Geometry & Probability**  
- **Topics**:  
  - Convex Hull (Graham’s Scan), Rotating Calipers.  
  - Line Sweep algorithms (closest pair of points).  
  - Probability basics (expected value, Markov chains).  
  - Randomized algorithms (Miller-Rabin primality test).  
- **Practice**:  
  - [LeetCode Geometry](https://leetcode.com/tag/geometry/) (e.g., 587, 963).  
  - [Kattis convexhull](https://open.kattis.com/problems/convexhull).
